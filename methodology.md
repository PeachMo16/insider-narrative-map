# Methodology

## 1. Data sources

This project uses public SEC filings, including Form 4, 8-K, DEF 14A, and related company disclosures. Price data is used for contextual visualization.

## 2. Insider transactions

Form 4 filings are used to identify insider sales, purchase activity, transaction price, share count, transaction value, and post-transaction ownership.

## 3. Corporate events

8-K filings and company announcements are used to identify acquisitions, leadership changes, financing events, name changes, and strategic pivots.

## 4. Insider reference zone

The insider reference zone is the price range where major insiders sold meaningful amounts of stock. It is not a price target. It is a behavioral reference zone.

## 5. Narrative alignment

The analysis asks whether insider behavior occurred before, during, or after a corporate narrative shift.
